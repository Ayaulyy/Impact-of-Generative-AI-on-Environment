# Four project dimensions

Proposed scope for team agreement.

| Dimension | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Review generative AI environmental impacts; focus quantitative analysis on energy consumption in verified datasets. Explore historical trends and forecasts or scenarios. Include carbon and water quantitatively only when suitable evidence exists. |
| Quality | Cite sources, verify units and provenance, distinguish measured/estimated/synthetic observations, document cleaning and label figures. Evaluate forecasts on later held-out data against a simple baseline. |
| Timing | Agree the course deadline and task dates. Complete the data audit before committing to forecasting. Reserve time for peer review and rehearsal. |
| Resources | Student team, existing computers, GitHub, accessible literature and datasets, and agreed analysis software. Assign owners and available hours. No paid resources assumed. |

## Deliverables

1. Dataset inventory and quality assessment.
2. Reproducible analysis and visualisations.
3. Evaluated forecast or transparent scenario analysis.
4. Presentation of findings, uncertainty and limitations.

The initial project-definition brief requests at most 3 slides or 2 pages. Confirm final-submission requirements separately.

## Boundaries

Regional electricity and gas trends alone cannot identify AI consumption or establish causality. Dataset titles do not establish verified coverage. A full lifecycle assessment is outside the initial scope.

## Decisions to confirm

- Research question and unit of analysis.
- Dataset selection and provenance.
- Forecasting feasibility and horizon.
- Owners, deadline, available hours and tools.
